LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the protection of your personal and financial information are important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Refund collects, uses, processes, stores, and protects information provided through our website and services.

Effective Date: November 17, 2026
Last Updated: November 17, 2026

Your information matters.

Refund handles personal identification information, financial records, and bank account details as sensitive information. Access to this information should be limited to authorized personnel who need it to perform their assigned responsibilities.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, submit a refund claim, request an investigation, or use our services, we may collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal Information

  • Full legal name.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Residential and mailing address.
  • Date of birth, where required for identity verification.
  • Account registration and profile information.

B. Identity Verification Documents

To establish account ownership, verify customer identity, prevent impersonation, and support legitimate refund claims, we may request identity verification documents, including:

  • Government-issued identification cards.
  • Passports and driver's licenses.
  • Other legally accepted identity documents.
  • Documents supporting ownership of a submitted claim.
  • Additional verification information where legally necessary.

C. Financial and Banking Information

Because our services involve refund claims and financial recovery processes, we may collect financial information necessary to investigate claims and process authorized payments.

  • Bank account holder names.
  • Bank names and account details.
  • Account numbers and routing information, where applicable.
  • Payment transaction references.
  • Records of deposits, transfers, and refunds.
  • Payment destination information.

We do not require customers to disclose their online banking passwords, PINs, one-time verification codes, or security credentials. Customers should never submit these credentials through our website.

D. Claim and Investigation Information

  • Details of the original financial transaction.
  • Information about the company or organization involved.
  • Payment receipts and transaction confirmations.
  • Supporting documents and correspondence.
  • Investigation notes and claim progress records.
  • Communications with our customer support team.

E. Technical Information

When you access our website, certain technical information may be collected, including IP address, browser type, device information, access timestamps, and website activity. Cookies or similar technologies may also be used where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through our services may be used for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Verifying customer identities and account ownership.
  • Reviewing and evaluating submitted refund claims.
  • Conducting claim investigations and maintaining case records.
  • Communicating investigation updates and account notifications.
  • Verifying financial transactions and payment destinations.
  • Processing authorized refunds and payments.
  • Detecting suspicious activity and preventing fraud.
  • Maintaining records required for legal and regulatory compliance.
  • Improving website security and service functionality.

3. How We Process Banking Information

Financial information is collected only where it is relevant to the services being requested or to the administration of a customer's claim.

Bank information may be used to verify payment records, establish the intended recipient of an authorized refund, reconcile financial transactions, and facilitate payment processing.

Access to banking information should be restricted to authorized personnel and approved service providers performing necessary financial or administrative functions.

Customers should ensure that submitted bank account details are accurate and belong to the intended payment recipient.

4. Identity Verification and Account Security

We may request identity documents before providing certain account services or processing a financial request.

Verification information helps us establish account ownership, reduce unauthorized access, and assess whether submitted claims are supported by the available documentation.

Verification does not guarantee that a claim will be successful or that funds will be recovered.

5. When We Share Your Information

Refund does not treat customer information as publicly available information. However, certain information may need to be shared for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

These recipients may include:

  • Authorized internal personnel: Employees and investigators who require access to relevant information to handle customer claims.
  • Financial institutions: Banks and payment service providers where necessary to verify or facilitate authorized transactions.
  • Technology providers: Hosting, database, security, and communication providers that support our services.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities: Where disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • Investigation partners: Relevant organizations where sharing information is lawful, necessary, and appropriately authorized.

We do not sell customers' personal identification documents or bank account information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

6. Data Storage and Security

We recognize the sensitive nature of identity and financial information. Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be maintained to protect customer data against unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Depending on the systems in use, these safeguards may include:

  • Access restrictions and role-based permissions.
  • Encryption during transmission and storage.
  • Secure authentication and account access controls.
  • Monitoring of relevant system activity.
  • Secure handling of uploaded identity documents.
  • Regular security reviews and system maintenance.

No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security of information transmitted or stored electronically.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including managing claims, maintaining financial records, resolving disputes, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, the status of a customer's claim, applicable regulatory requirements, and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable law and our retention procedures.

8. International Data Transfers

Some of our technology providers or service partners may operate in countries other than the country in which a customer resides.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, appropriate safeguards and applicable legal requirements must be followed to protect the information.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format.
  • Submit a complaint to the appropriate data protection authority.

Some requests may be limited by legal, financial, regulatory, or recordkeeping obligations.

10. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain sessions, improve website functionality, remember preferences, and understand website performance.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

11. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on external service providers for hosting, authentication, database management, payment processing, analytics, and communication.

These providers may process certain information on our behalf under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We encourage customers to review the privacy practices of relevant third-party services.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal or financial information from children through our services.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, information-processing practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice of material changes.

14. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your privacy rights, or have concerns about the handling of your personal or financial information, please contact us.
