OUR COMMITMENT

Clarity over promises.

We won't present a case as successful before a result is verified, or imply that submitting a request guarantees money back.

Case status should reflect recorded information.
Financial figures should be clearly explained.
Decisions should have a traceable record.

These are service principles, not third-party certifications or a claim of government affiliation.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Understand the process before you decide how to proceed.

Still have questions? →
Does submitting a claim guarantee a refund?

No. A request must be assessed, and any outcome depends on the facts, available evidence, applicable rules, and the organizations involved.

What if a company referred me?

Use the designated partner referral route or link provided by that organization. Referred customers may follow a different onboarding process from direct claimants.

Can a recovery arrive in installments?

Some recovery processes may involve more than one payment. The dashboard should distinguish amounts verified as received from amounts that are still expected or outstanding.

When can I request recovered funds?

Under the current planned process, payout requests become available after the full recovery amount has been verified. Applicable checks and administrative processing may still be required.

How do I know a communication is genuine?

Use the company's official website and published contact channels to verify unexpected messages. Be cautious of anyone demanding extra payment with a promise of guaranteed recovery.
